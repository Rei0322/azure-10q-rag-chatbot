# Tested Prompts

Prompts developed and tested against The Walt Disney Company's Form 10-Q for the quarter ended June 27, 2026, using the `SEC-Chatbot` agent (`gpt-4.1-mini` with Azure AI Search retrieval).

| # | Category | Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Financial Performance | Summarize Disney's key financial metrics for the quarter ended June 27, 2026, including total revenue, net income, and diluted earnings per share, compared to the prior-year quarter. |
| 2 | Financial Performance | Which business segment had the largest increase in operating income this quarter, and what drove the increase? |
| 3 | Business Operations | What important events or strategic changes does Disney report this quarter, such as acquisitions, transactions, or new initiatives? |
| 4 | Risk Factors | What risks does Disney disclose in this 10-Q, especially regarding programming contracts, intellectual property, and AI? |
| 5 | Management Discussion | How does management explain the decrease in net income compared to the prior-year quarter, and what challenges do they identify? |

## Coverage

- **Financial Performance:** Prompts 1 and 2
- **Business Operations:** Prompt 3
- **Risk Factors:** Prompt 4
- **Management Discussion:** Prompt 5
